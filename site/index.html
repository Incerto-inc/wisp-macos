Record the meeting.
Keep each voice separate.

Wisp is a free, open-source recorder that lives in your Mac’s menu bar. Pick a window, a display or a single browser tab, and Wisp saves the video, your microphone and everyone else in the call as three separate files. Nothing leaves your Mac.

How a recording goes

  1. Open the panel

    In a Zoom, Teams, Meet or Webex call, the meeting is already picked, with the whole screen beside it for when you present.

  2. Pick anything else

    Every real window and display, and the tabs of Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Dia and Safari. Search narrows it down.

  3. Record

    A thin vermilion frame marks what is being captured. It never appears in the recording or in anyone’s screen share.

Three files, one timeline

The tracks start and stop together, so they line up to the frame.

Your voice and the other side are never mixed into one file. Transcripts come out cleaner, and who said what is settled before any software has to guess. Hand the folder to a transcription tool or an AI assistant as it is.

Built for real meetings

Tabs without an extension
Wisp asks the browser for its tabs through macOS Automation, once per browser. While the tab you picked is showing, its window is recorded; when you switch to another tab, the video holds its last frame so other tabs stay out. Drag the tab into a new window and Wisp follows it.
Sound follows the call
The system track records the meeting app whatever the video shows, so you can present slides and still capture the other side.
Survives a crash
Files are written in 10-second pieces. If the Mac crashes or loses power, everything up to the last piece still plays.
Says when something stops
If screen capture, system audio or the microphone stops in the middle of a meeting, the panel tells you right away.
Room mode
Microphone only, for a laptop left on the table in a meeting room.
Keyboard first
Return starts, ⌘. stops, Esc goes back or closes the panel.

Nothing leaves your Mac.

Wisp makes no network requests. There is no account, no analytics and no update check. Recordings stay in your Movies folder.

Meeting detection reads app names, window titles and tab addresses on your Mac, and nowhere else.

This page doesn’t load anything from anyone else, either.

Install

For now Wisp is built from source; a signed download will follow. You need macOS 14 or later and Xcode with Swift 6.

  1. Get the code

    git clone https://github.com/Incerto-inc/wisp-macos.git
cd wisp-macos

  2. Build and install

    ./Scripts/package-app.sh

    This builds a release version and installs it as /Applications/Wisp.app.

  3. Allow recording

    Open Wisp from the menu bar and allow Screen Recording and Microphone when macOS asks. Each browser asks once, the first time you show its tabs.
